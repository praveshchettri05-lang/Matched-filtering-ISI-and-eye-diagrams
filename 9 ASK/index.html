Welcome to the ASK & BFSK Simulator!

This interactive tool helps you understand Digital Modulation techniques.

  • Waveforms: See how digital bits (0s and 1s) are converted into analog signals.
  • Power Spectra: Visualize the frequency content and bandwidth of the modulated signals.
  • Correlator Outputs: Observe how the receiver interprets the incoming signal.
  • Decision Histograms: See how noise causes the receiver's decisions to blur together.
  • BER Curves: The ultimate performance metric - Bit Error Rate vs. Signal-to-Noise Ratio.
  • Orthogonality: See how the frequency spacing between tones affects BFSK performance.

How to use:

Use the sidebar controls on the left to tweak parameters like bit rate, carrier frequencies, and noise level (Eb/N0). The graphs will update automatically!

If the graphs seem squished, just scroll down to see the rest of them.
